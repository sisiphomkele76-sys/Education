About the Project

The HIV Education & Medication Support System was developed by Sisipho Mkele and facilitated by Nyakama Mahle under the Make Me A Coder programme.

Our mission is to break language and medical terminology barriers across the globe, providing accessible, confidential HIV education, testing resources, prescribed medication guides, and time reminders.
